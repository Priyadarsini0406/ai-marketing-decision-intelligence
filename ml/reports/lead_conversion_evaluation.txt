Decision-Intel Lead Conversion Prediction Experiment
=======================================================
Project: Education Lead Conversion (X Education leads dataset)
Objective: Predict the probability that an education lead converts, then
rank leads as High / Medium / Low priority for follow-up.

Dataset
-------
- Path: data/Leads X Education.csv
- Rows: 9240
- Columns: 37
- Target: Converted (0 = not converted, 1 = converted)
- Class distribution: {0: 5679, 1: 3561}
- Duplicate rows: 0
- Missing values: 41039

Features used (18)
------------------------------
Numeric (5): TotalVisits, Total Time Spent on Website, Page Views Per Visit, Asymmetrique Activity Score, Asymmetrique Profile Score
Categorical (13): Lead Origin, Lead Source, Do Not Email, Last Activity, Country, Specialization, How did you hear about X Education, What is your current occupation, City, Asymmetrique Activity Index, Asymmetrique Profile Index, A free copy of Mastering The Interview, Last Notable Activity

Features excluded and why
--------------------------
- Identifiers (2): Prospect ID, Lead Number
- Constant columns (5): Magazine, Receive More Updates About Our Courses, Update me on Supply Chain Content, Get updates on DM Content, I agree to pay the amount through cheque
- Post-hoc leakage labels (3): Tags, Lead Quality, Lead Profile (assigned after contact; leak outcome)
- Near-constant columns (8): Do Not Call, Search, Newspaper Article, X Education Forums, Newspaper, Digital Advertisement, Through Recommendations, What matters most to you in choosing a course (minority < 0.2%; no signal)

Preprocessing
-------------
- 'Select' placeholder mapped to 'Not Selected'; missing categoricals -> 'Missing'
- Numeric: median imputation + StandardScaler
- Categorical: constant imputation + OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore')
- Train/test split before fitting any transformation (no data leakage)
- Split: 80/20 stratified, random_state=42
- Class imbalance (38.5% converted) handled with balanced class weights

Model results (held-out test set)
---------------------------------
Model | Accuracy | Precision | Recall | F1 | ROC-AUC
Logistic Regression | 0.8160 | 0.7391 | 0.8076 | 0.7718 | 0.8980
Random Forest | 0.8258 | 0.7546 | 0.8118 | 0.7821 | 0.9051
Gradient Boosting | 0.8360 | 0.7632 | 0.8329 | 0.7965 | 0.9127
XGBoost | 0.8447 | 0.7785 | 0.8343 | 0.8054 | 0.9163

Selected model: XGBoost
Selection basis: highest F1 for the positive (converted) class, the business
metric used to find leads worth following up, with ROC-AUC as tie-breaker.
